Crop insurance and farmers' income

An independent research project, 2025 to 2026, on how crop insurance affects farmers' income and livelihood.

  • Designed a questionnaire and collected primary data from 150 farmers
  • Applied descriptive statistics, frequency analysis and correlation testing in SPSS
